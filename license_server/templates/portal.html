🔑 Activate (first time only)

  1. Download and run TropicChecker.exe
  2. Copy your Hardware ID from the activation screen and send it to your vendor
  3. Your vendor creates a license locked to your PC only and sends you an activation code like TROPIC-XXXX-XXXX
  4. In the app, open the Online tab, paste your code, and click Activate Online

Each license is locked to one PC. Need a new machine? Ask your vendor to reset your license.
