          Classification of Diabetes Mellitus (DM)
          • Type 1 DM: caused by autoimmune destruction of beta cells; absolute insulin deficiency
          • Type 2 DM: caused by the progressive loss of insulin secretion by beta cells, often with
            associated insulin resistance
          • Gestational DM: diabetes that develops during pregnancy that was not present prior to
            gestation, diagnosed in the 2nd or 3rd trimester
          • Others: latent autoimmune diabetes in adults (LADA), mild obesity-related diabetes (MOD),
            mild age-related diabetes (MARD)

          Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH)
          • TSH is the most sensitive measure of thyroid function.
            $ TSH is the “early warning system”.
            $ Changes in TSH levels occur before changes in T3/T4.
            $ More reliable than T3 and T4 levels
            $ A small change in circulating T3/T4 results in a very large change in TSH.
          • TSH measurement is clinically useful.
            $ To screen for thyroid disorders
            $ To monitor thyroid hormone supplementation

Subclinical Hypothyroidism
• Labs: increased TSH, normal free T4
  $ TSH greater than 10 mU/L
    • Treat to prevent progression to primary hypothyroidism.
    • Prescribe 25 to 50 mcg or 1.6 mcg/kg/day IBW.
  $ TSH less than 10 mU/L
    • Treatment usually not beneficial
• Recheck TSH in 6 to 12 months or if symptoms increase.

Patient Education About Levothyroxine
• Levothyroxine is a picky drug!
  $ Must take with water on an empty stomach
  $ Interacts with other medications
• Hypothyroidism control affected by
  $ Weight change of 10% or more
  $ Taking levothyroxine with food or other meds
  $ Missed or extra medication doses
  $ Use of antiepileptic drugs, steroids
  $ Pregnancy (increases needs 30%)
  $ Biotin interferes with measurement of TSH
