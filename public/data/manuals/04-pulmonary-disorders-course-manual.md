NOTES     Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP)
          • Acquired outside of hospital!
          • High morbidity and mortality
            $ Risk factors
              • Older age
              • Chronic comorbidities
              • Viral respiratory infections
              • Impaired airway protection
              • Smoking, alcohol excess
              • Environmental factors
                 $ Toxins
                 $ Social determinants of health


          Inpatient vs. Outpatient Treatment
          CURB-65: predicts mortality
          • C = Confusion
          • U = BUN >19 mg/dL
          • R = Respiratory rate ≥30
          • B = Blood pressure (SBP <90 or DBP <60 mm Hg)
          • Age ≥65 years
          CURB-65 Score Recommendation
          0–1           Low risk; consider outpatient treatment

          2             Brief inpatient or close monitoring of outpatient treatment

          3–5           Requires inpatient treatment

Disease                 Characteristics

                        years (may develop COPD)

Pulmonary embolism      Risk factors; vague symptoms for weeks; tachypnea, rales, dyspnea, pleuritic

NOTES     COPD Symptom Tools
          CAT assessment: Rates symptoms on a Likert scale from 0–5
          Subjective symptoms of:
          • cough, mucus, chest tightness, breathlessness, limited activities, confidence leaving home,
            sleep, level of energy
          MMRC assessment:
          Grade     Description of Breathlessness
          0         I only get breathless with strenuous exercise.

          1         I get short of breath when hurrying on level ground or walking up a slight hill.

          2         On level ground, I walk slower than people of the same age because of breathlessness, or I must
                    stop for breath when walking at my own pace.

          3         I stop for breath after walking about 100 yards or after a few minutes on level ground.

          4         I am too breathless to leave the house, or I am breathless when dressing.
