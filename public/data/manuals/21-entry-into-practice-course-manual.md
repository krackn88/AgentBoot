Expired Certification                                                                                          NOTES
• Cease and desist!
  $ Using the title
  $ Practicing

  $ Threatens reimbursement


Still Deciding?

Expired Certification
ANCC:
• <2 years: reactivate by renewal requirements
• ≥2 years: Complete 75 CE, retest, and pass certification exam (if not retired exam).
AANP: Complete renewal requirements.
Best practice: Renew 6 months before expiration.


Maintaining Certification
• Current active/unencumbered license                  • Renewal/recertification handbooks

• Keep supporting documents                            • Notify BON of recertification.
  (e.g., CE certificates).

                            BECOMING CERTIFIED AND LICENSED

          Testing Center
          Location nearest to you: research location
          • Arrive 30 min before scheduled testing time.
          • Two forms of identification (one photo)
            $ Name must match application
            $ Temporary or expired IDs are NOT acceptable
            $ Must contain signature
            $ Bring eligibility letter or ATT
          • If late, missed scheduled appointment time, or arrive without proper ID:
            $ Will not be able to take their examination as scheduled.
            $ Will be responsible for paying any applicable testing center fees.
            $ Will forfeit their fee and require a new registration number.


          Testing Center: Test Day!
          • Scratch paper provided; dry erase board
          • Locker to store personal items
          • No scheduled breaks
          • No misconduct


          Did I Pass?
          • Immediate results
          • Detailed score report mailed (AANP/ANCC); emailed
          • Certificate 3–4 weeks
          • How are exams scored?
            $ Criterion-referenced examinations
            $ Scaled scoring systems
            $ Total number of correct answers, converted into consistent and standardized scale
            $ Provides direct and fair comparisons


          Retesting
          • Must apply to retest
          • Waiting period
          • Limited number of times to retest
            $ ANCC three times in 12 months (certified within 5 years)
            $ AANP two times in a calendar year
          • Eligibility to retest/retest fee
            Specific criteria:
            $ Continuing education
            $ Wait until next testing window
            $ Certifying body/state limitations

                                    SCOPE OF PRACTICE

Bridges’ Transition Model: Endings to Beginnings
                  Transition is experienced as a time of chaos, turmoil, and confusion.
                     Process                                 Characteristics
Endings              Changing identities                        Uncertainty, insecurity, chaos

Neutral              Reflection                                 Reinvention, dynamic change, and growth

Beginnings           Changing relationships, new beginnings     Adaptation, adjustment

Defining attributes of transition from RN to NP using Bridges’ theory (2003)
Source: McLellan L, Levett-Jones T, Higgins I. Nurse practitioner role transition: a concept analysis. JAANP.
2015;27(7), 389–397.

Points of Negotiation                                                                                       NOTES
• Continuing education
• Professional memberships
• Licensure fees
• Prescriptive fees
• Communication (e.g., phone service)
• Insurance (e.g., malpractice)
• Professional needs (e.g., books, lab coats)
• Promotion of practice/NP


Methods of Payment
• Straight salary: fixed amount to perform according to the job description
• Percentage of net receipts: payment based on the amount the NP bills, minus accounts
  receivable, minus the NP’s portion of practice expenses (which may include expense of
  physician consultation)
• Base salary plus percentage: base salary guaranteed; additional salary may be earned if income
  over set amount is generated
• Hourly rate: payment made only for hours worked


Typical Policy Coverage
• $1 million each claim
• Amount of a professional liability claim
• You are legally obligated to pay
• A covered medical incident
• $3 million annual aggregate, for all covered claims during the policy period


Two Main Types of Policies
