                                           PRACTICE QUESTIONS

             Question banks with rationales

                               Read the question, picking out relevant
                               facts (e.g., age, most likely, first-line).

                               Try to answer the question without looking
                               at the answer choices.

                               Read the answer choices and narrow down
                               the options.


                               Reread the question.


                               Answer or make your best guess!


             Labs to Commit to Memory
             TSH: 0.5-4 mIU/L
             PSA: <4.0 ng/mL
             Lipids: goals based on cardiovascular risk e.g., moderate CV risk: LDL-C <100 mg/dL
             Glucose: fasting: 70–100 mg/dL; Know levels to diagnose diabetes
             A1c: <5.7 %; know diagnostic criteria and target goals
             eGFR: ≥90 mL/min/1.73m2; know expectations for age and renal staging
             INR: know therapeutic levels (see Cardio module)
             CBC, CMP: know the general normal values. For example, a potassium level of 6.0 mEq/L is
             grossly abnormal.

Weeks/Days Before the Exam                                                                                   NOTES
• 10–14 days prior, take a predictor (if desired).
• Visit the testing center (traffic patterns, center location, noises).
• Gather all the items needed for the day of the exam.
  $ Authorization to test
  $ Photo ID
• Self-care strategies
  $ Be CONFIDENT!! Visualize success!
  $ Practice positive affirmations.
  $ Protect personal time.
  $ Prioritize sleep.


Day of Exam
• Conserve mental energy.
  $ 1–2 min break every hour
• Never assume information that is not given.
• All information needed to answer the questions is provided.
• Use the question answering technique.
• Answer ALL questions!
• Be realistic: Don’t expect to know everything.
• Breathe!!
