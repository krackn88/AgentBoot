      APEA Resources
      Hollier’s Clinical Guidelines in Primary Care, 5th edition: Written and reviewed by nurse practitioner experts,
      this evidence-based text is valued and trusted by faculty, students, and practicing NPs. Organized for optimal
      learning and practice, the chapters are structured around key body systems and common primary care
      diagnoses. Each chapter outlines the standard of care process including assessment, diagnosis, planning,
      implementation, and evaluation. A premier resource to enhance your clinical reasoning and critical thinking
      skills.
      CareOnPoint: A mobile clinical reference that electronically delivers 100% of the diagnoses contained in
      Hollier’s Clinical Guidelines in Primary Care, 5th edition, plus additional diagnoses and images! Earn contact
      hours while you use this resource! This product is sold in 1-year and 2-year subscriptions.
      MyQBank: Practicing in MyQBank Management is the most effective way to prepare for the AANP and ANCC
      certification exams. Each question includes detailed rationales that cover essential diagnoses and domains.
      You can practice in tutor mode—answer one question at a time and review the rationale—or in test mode to
      simulate the real exam experience. Missed questions are flagged so you can revisit them. Flexible filtering
      lets you target specific knowledge areas (e.g., cardiovascular) or build random sets to match your study
      plan. Choose from eight question banks designed to help you master subject areas fundamental to your NP
      education and certification; specialty banks (e.g. MyQBank Prescribing, MyQBank Pediatrics, etc.) are also
      available for tailored preparation.
      Certification Questions, 4th edition: This print book offers the same high-quality questions in an easy-to-use
      format. Each chapter begins with “focus tables” that highlight key content. Clinical pearls of wisdom and
      strategies to strengthen study habits are provided throughout. This book emphasizes the development of
      clinical reasoning and critical thinking skills that are essential for both exam success and clinical practice.
      Predictor Exam: Offers 150 randomly selected questions designed to give you the experience of a mock
      certification exam that is psychometrically sound and statistically predictive. This one-time exam mirrors the
      ANCC or AANP certification exams (FNP or AGPCNP) by testing across key domains and diagnoses. The exam
      report correlates knowledge areas with testing domains, thus pinpointing knowledge deficits as they relate to
      diagnoses. Because the Predictor Exam simulates the actual certification exam, rationales and answers are not
      provided, but the detailed score report predicts likelihood of first-time success, guides study planning, and
      helps determine the time needed for remediation before the real exam.

                                            COURSE OBJECTIVES
      After completion of this activity, participants will be able to:
      1. Demonstrate Comprehensive Clinical Knowledge through the application and reinforcement of evidence-
          based knowledge in primary care.
      2. Enhance Diagnostic and Clinical Reasoning Skills by performing accurate assessments, formulating
          differential diagnoses, and developing effective treatment plans.
      3. Prepare for Certification Success: Participants will be equipped with test-taking strategies and a thorough
          understanding of the FNP (or AGPCNP) Certification exam format and content, increasing readiness and
          confidence.
      4. Promote Professional Competency and Role Transition: Support the transition to advanced practice
          through review of scope of practice, ethical considerations, and interprofessional collaboration.
