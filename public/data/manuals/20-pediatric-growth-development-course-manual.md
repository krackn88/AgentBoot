          Vision Screening in Children
          • Once a child develops conjugate gaze (older than
            6 months), they should be screened for strabismus.
            $ Strabismus: deviation of one eye due to
              oculomotor weakness ➔ amblyopia
            $ Amblyopia: loss of vision in one eye
              • When the two eyes don’t focus on the same object, the brain ignores information from one
                 of the eyes.

           SPORTS PREPARTICIPATION EVALUATION (PPE)

Sports PPE
• Most children participate in a sport at some point
  during their school years.
• Perform 4–6 weeks before participation.
• Provide age-appropriate anticipatory guidance.

Visit Goals
• Preparticipation evaluations (PPE) have several goals:
  primary goal is safe participation!
  $ Recognize children who might experience life-
     threatening complications from participation.
  $ Identify children with medical problems that must
     be addressed to allow for safe participation.
  $ Intervene and rehab children with history of musculoskeletal problems.
  $ Optimize health conditions that may be exacerbated by activity.


Components
• Standard evaluation forms have been             • Assess medical history:
  endorsed by several organizations, including      $ Immunizations
  American Academy of Pediatrics.
                                                    $ Medications
  $ School-specific forms
                                                    $ Menstrual history
  $ Organization-specific form
                                                    $ Weight changes
• Medical history should be completed by
  parents and child together if possible.

Medical History: Cardiac Concerns
• Past/current cardiac conditions                 • Family history
• Syncope and heat-related events                   $ Heart disease
• Symptoms                                          $ Unexplained sudden death
  $ Fatigue                                         $ Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
  $ Shortness of breath                             $ Inheritable arrhythmias
  $ Chest pain
• Abnormal cardiac testing


Physical Exam: Cardiovascular
• Vital signs: look for elevated blood pressure, height, weight
  $ Body composition is measured for wrestling if low weight.
• Auscultation: murmurs, gallops, rubs
• Femoral pulses: should be equal (if unequal, consider
  coarctation of the aorta)
  REFER!!
